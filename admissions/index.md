    <h2 id="undergraduate">B.Tech Admissions Procedure</h2>
    <p>
      Admissions to the 4-year Bachelor of Technology program are conducted through the National Engineering Entrance Examination rank list followed by centralized counseling.
    </p>
    <h3>Eligibility Criteria:</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Completion of 10+2 higher secondary examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.</li>
      <li>Minimum 75% aggregate marks (or top 20 percentile of the respective state/national board).</li>
      <li>Valid qualification score in the national Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced).</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 id="postgraduate">M.Tech & M.S. Admissions</h2>
    <p>
      Candidates holding a recognized B.Tech/B.E. degree in Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, or allied engineering branches with a valid national GATE score are eligible to apply.
    </p>
    <h3>Selection Process:</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>Shortlisting based on GATE percentiles (70% weightage).</li>
      <li>Written technical aptitude test & departmental interview (30% weightage).</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
