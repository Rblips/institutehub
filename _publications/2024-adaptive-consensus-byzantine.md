### Abstract

Geo-distributed BFT systems suffer high latency due to cross-region round trips and varying WAN congestion. We propose GeoBFT-Adapt, an adaptive leader-election and pipelined batch commit protocol that dynamically adjusts quorum sizing according to real-time latency matrix measurements, reducing P99 latency by up to 48% across global testbeds.
