### Abstract

This paper proposes an asynchronous consensus-based exploration algorithm for heterogeneous ground and aerial robotic teams in GPS-denied, communication-intermittent underground environments. Through ultra-wideband mesh ranging and localized probabilistic occupancy grids, the swarm achieves complete map convergence 3.2x faster than classical frontier-based methods with zero centralized coordination.
