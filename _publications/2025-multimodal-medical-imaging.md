### Abstract

Clinical deployment of deep learning models requires verifiable explanations correlating volumetric imaging patterns with patient laboratory records. We introduce a self-supervised cross-attention architecture that projects multi-slice chest CT features and clinical metadata into a shared concept space. The model generates anatomically grounded heatmap evidence masks, achieving a 0.94 AUROC on acute respiratory distress classification while providing certified clinician interpretability.
