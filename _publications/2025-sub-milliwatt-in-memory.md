### Abstract

Deploying generative and multi-head attention models onto energy-constrained edge sensors is strictly limited by the energy required to transfer weights across memory buses. This paper presents a fabricated 28nm ReRAM macro implementing 8-bit analog matrix operations within the crossbar array itself. Our prototype delivers a measured 31.4 TOPS/W energy efficiency at 0.7V supply, demonstrating full sentence transformer embeddings at sub-milliwatt dissipation.
