### Abstract

Topological surface codes present the highest fault-tolerance threshold for physical qubits. We demonstrate a rotated planar surface code requiring 35% fewer auxiliary ancilla measurement operations per syndrome cycle, utilizing dynamic decoding graphs executed on FPGA controllers at sub-microsecond latencies.
