### Abstract

This paper presents IndicBridge, a parameter-efficient adapter framework for multilingual LLMs that leverages syntactic and phonetic commonalities across Indo-Aryan and Dravidian language families. IndicBridge yields +8.4 BLEU improvements over standard zero-shot baselines across 12 low-resource translation and summarization tasks.
