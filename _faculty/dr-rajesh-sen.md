Dr. Rajesh Sen specializes in modern operating system architecture, hypervisors, and capability-based memory safety. His laboratory—the Systems Architecture and Resilience (SAR) Lab—focuses on Linux kernel hardening, type-safe kernel extensions, and microkernel verification for confidential computing enclaves.
