Dr. Aravind Nair is a Professor and Chair of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at InstituteHub. His research group investigates the foundational and architectural challenges of large-scale distributed systems, specifically geo-distributed state replication, asynchronous consensus protocols, and resilient infrastructure for mission-critical cloud deployments.

Before joining InstituteHub in 2014, Dr. Nair held research scientist appointments at Bell Labs and worked closely with industrial consortiums developing fault-tolerant storage architectures. He has authored over 60 peer-reviewed articles in IEEE and ACM venues and serves on the editorial board of international distributed computing journals.
