Dr. Meera Krishnamurthy directs the Center for Frontier Artificial Intelligence at InstituteHub. Her primary research interests span deep learning architectures for high-dimensional visual perception, robust semantic segmentation, and explainable neural representations in healthcare diagnostics.

She has served as Area Chair for CVPR, ICCV, and NeurIPS. Her laboratory collaborates extensively with national medical research institutes to deploy real-time diagnostic AI models on edge hospital hardware.
