    <h2 id="overview">Institutional Overview & History</h2>
    <p>
      Established in 1998, <strong>InstituteHub of Technology & Engineering</strong> was founded with a singular mission: to cultivate world-class engineers, computer scientists, and researchers capable of architecting critical infrastructure and solving complex societal challenges.
    </p>
    <p>
      Over the past quarter-century, the institute has expanded from a specialized computing center into an internationally acclaimed research powerhouse. Spread across a state-of-the-art 180-acre smart campus in Technopolis, InstituteHub accommodates over 8,000 students, 150 full-time faculty members, 40 specialized research centers, and over 280 granted technological patents.
    </p>

      <ul style="margin: 0; padding-left: 1.25rem;">
        <li><strong>Rigor & Excellence:</strong> Deliver uncompromising academic curricula combining foundational mathematical rigor with hands-on systems engineering.</li>
        <li><strong>Frontier Research:</strong> Pursue high-impact interdisciplinary research in systems, artificial intelligence, quantum sciences, and sustainable technologies.</li>
        <li><strong>Societal Contribution:</strong> Bridge the gap between academia and industrial application through active technology transfer and entrepreneurship.</li>
        <li><strong>Inclusivity & Ethics:</strong> Foster an open, diverse, and collaborative scholarly community dedicated to sustainable and ethical technology deployment.</li>

    <h2 id="values">Core Institutional Values</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Intellectual Integrity:</strong> Uncompromising dedication to scientific honesty, peer review, and academic freedom.</li>
      <li><strong>Engineering Rigor:</strong> Commitment to building functional, robust, and mathematically verified systems rather than superficial abstractions.</li>
      <li><strong>Sustainability:</strong> Embedding ecological responsibility directly into engineering designs, cleanroom operations, and campus infrastructure.</li>
      <li><strong>Collaboration:</strong> Interdisciplinary synergy between computer science, electrical systems, physics, and robotics disciplines.</li>
    </ul>
