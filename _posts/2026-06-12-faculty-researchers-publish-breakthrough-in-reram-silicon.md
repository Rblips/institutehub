In a paper published in the *IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits*, an InstituteHub research team led by Dr. Ananya Deshmukh demonstrated a functioning resistive RAM (ReRAM) compute-in-memory accelerator that runs transformer language attention layers at 31.4 TOPS/Watt.

The breakthrough circumvents the memory-bandwidth bottleneck that has historically prevented transformer neural networks from running on ultra-compact, battery-less edge sensors.
