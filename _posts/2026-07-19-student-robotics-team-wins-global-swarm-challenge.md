Competing against 42 university teams from across North America, Europe, and Asia, Team SwarmForge from InstituteHub won First Place and the Innovation Trophy at the International Swarm Autonomy Challenge.

Mentored by Dr. Priya Ramanathan, the 8-student team demonstrated an autonomous fleet of micro-drones capable of navigating through dense artificial smoke and dense forest obstacles to localize multiple acoustic beacons in under 4 minutes.
