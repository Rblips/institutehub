At a formal ceremony held in New Delhi, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology awarded Dr. Meera Krishnamurthy, Director of InstituteHub's Center for Frontier AI, the National AI Excellence Award for 2026.

Dr. Krishnamurthy's research group has pioneered deep neural architectures that diagnose acute radiological conditions with high sensitivity while generating mathematically verifiable interpretability heatmaps for attending physicians. The technology is currently deployed in clinical research trials across three major teaching hospitals.
