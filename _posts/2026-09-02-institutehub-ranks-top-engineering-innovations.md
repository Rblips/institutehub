The National Engineering and Research Council released its 2026 Institutional Quality Metrics, placing InstituteHub among the top 5 premier engineering institutes in the country. The comprehensive assessment evaluates academic citations, sponsored research funding, patent commercialization, student faculty ratios, and graduate employment outcomes.

Speaking on this accomplishment, the Institute Director stated:
> "This milestone reflects the persistent dedication of our faculty, researchers, and bright students who continually push the boundaries of frontier engineering. Our commitment remains steadfast on translational research that transforms society."

The report specifically commended InstituteHub's recent establishment of the Center for Frontier Artificial Intelligence and the Sustainable Microgrid Research Facility.
