The Office of Sponsored Research and Industrial Consultancy announced a major collaboration with five leading international telecommunications providers to install an open-architecture 5G standalone core and 6G experimental sub-terahertz testbed.

Led by Dr. Vikram Singh, the testbed will provide undergraduate and postgraduate researchers direct access to carrier-grade millimeter-wave radios, software-defined basebands, and P4-programmable switching fabrics.
