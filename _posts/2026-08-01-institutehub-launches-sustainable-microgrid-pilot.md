Under the leadership of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, InstituteHub has commissioned a 2.5 MW distributed solar photovoltaic and 5 MWh energy storage system across academic rooftop structures.

The installation operates on algorithms developed by Dr. Rohit Banerjee's research group, utilizing real-time cloud-forecasting telemetry and grid-forming inverter controls that allow the campus to seamlessly island from the municipal grid during power disruptions.
