Under a memorandum of understanding signed today, InstituteHub's Systems Architecture and Resilience Lab will partner with national cybersecurity authorities to evaluate real-time threat-hunting architectures and zero-trust protocol isolation in mission-critical infrastructure testbeds.

Dr. Suresh Iyer and Dr. Devika Pillai will co-lead the joint operational testing initiative on campus.
