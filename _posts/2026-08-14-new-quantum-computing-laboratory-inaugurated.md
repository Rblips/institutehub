InstituteHub expanded its physical research footprint with the inauguration of the Bose Quantum Systems Laboratory. Built at an investment of INR 35 Crores under the National Quantum Mission, the new complex will serve as the testbed for developing fault-tolerant surface codes and cryogenic control controllers.

The facility features millikelvin dilution refrigerators capable of maintaining superconducting test chips at 10 mK, alongside sub-microsecond syndrome decoding testbenches engineered by InstituteHub graduate scholars.
