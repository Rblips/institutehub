InstituteHub held its 24th Annual Convocation on campus, conferring degrees upon 780 B.Tech graduates, 340 M.Tech/M.S. postgraduates, and 85 doctoral scholars. The Chief Guest, a renowned pioneer in space engineering systems, urged graduates to apply their technical skills to climate resilience, ethical computational systems, and inclusive infrastructure.

President's Gold Medals were awarded to top-ranking graduates across Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, and Robotics.
