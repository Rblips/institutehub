The Department of Computer Science and Engineering organized the 2026 Open Systems Sprint, bringing together core maintainers from upstream Linux kernel subsystems, LLVM compiler toolchains, and distributed database ecosystems. Over 180 pull requests and patches were merged upstream during the 3-day hackathon.
