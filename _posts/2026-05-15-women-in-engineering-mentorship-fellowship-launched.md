InstituteHub launched the *Ada Lovelace Fellowship for Women in Advanced Engineering*, endowed by distinguished alumni and philanthropic corporate partners. The program provides full doctoral tuition remission, competitive living stipends, and structured research internships at global industrial research laboratories.
