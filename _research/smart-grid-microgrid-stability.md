Traditional electrical grids rely on the physical inertia of heavy rotating thermal turbines to stabilize frequency. As solar photovoltaics and wind turbines replace thermal power, grid inertia approaches zero, resulting in severe vulnerability to voltage collapse.

This project delivers decentralized virtual synchronous generator control algorithms validated on a multi-megawatt hardware-in-the-loop testbed, proving microgrid islanding and seamless black-start capabilities.
