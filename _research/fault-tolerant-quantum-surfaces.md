The primary hurdle toward practical quantum advantage is environmental thermal noise and physical qubit decoherence.

The **Fault-Tolerant Surface Code Project** designs high-threshold lattice codes and sub-microsecond neural syndrome decoders operating on FPGA cryo-controllers, allowing thousands of error syndromes to be corrected within the coherent physical lifetime of superconducting qubits.
