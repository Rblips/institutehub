Artificial intelligence training clusters and disaggregated memory systems place extreme throughput and deterministic microsecond latency demands on datacenter switching fabrics.

Our team develops in-network telemetry (INT) feedback mechanisms where switches compute path congestion in hardware pipelines at 400 Gbps line rates and proactively reshape sender queues before buffer packet drops occur.
