While frontier large language models demonstrate impressive capabilities in English and high-resource European languages, their performance degrades drastically when handling syntactically complex, agglutinative, and morphologically rich languages.

The **Indic Multilingual LLM Initiative** builds open-source linguistic benchmarks, morphologically-aware subword tokenizers, and instruction-tuning datasets across Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Kannada, and other regional tongues.
