In disaster zones such as earthquakes or structural collapses, external communication infrastructure is destroyed and GPS signals cannot penetrate subterranean environments.

Our project deploys swarms of micro-UAVs (drones) and tracked ground rovers that share lightweight topological point-cloud maps peer-to-peer using directional ultra-wideband mesh radios, dynamically partitioning search areas to locate survivors in minutes.
