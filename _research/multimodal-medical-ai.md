Clinical diagnosis requires simultaneous synthesis of unstructured radiological scans (CT, MRI, X-rays) alongside structured electronic health records (EHR) and clinical notes. Current proprietary deep learning models remain black boxes, limiting their adoption in high-stakes clinical workflows.

This project formulates self-supervised multimodal representation pipelines that yield localized spatial attention saliency maps aligned with validated anatomical ontologies. Our clinical pilots at partner teaching hospitals have demonstrated significant improvements in early detection sensitivity for pulmonary and neurological anomalies.
