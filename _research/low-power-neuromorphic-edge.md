Conventional deep learning processors consume tens to hundreds of watts, making them unusable for battery-operated environmental sensing and wearable medical patches.

This project implements compute-in-memory (CIM) macro arrays where vector-matrix multiplications are executed in parallel directly inside the memory array using Kirchhoff’s current laws, achieving an energy efficiency exceeding 30 TOPS/Watt.
