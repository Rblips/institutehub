The Office of Sponsored Research & Industrial Consultancy (SRIC) announces the launch of the Research Seed Grant (RSG) scheme for the academic session 2026-27. Seed grants of up to **INR 25 Lakhs** per faculty member are provided to initiate exploratory laboratory facilities, procure instrumentation, and hire initial project fellows.

**Proposal Submission Deadline:** 20th September 2026 via the internal R&D portal.
