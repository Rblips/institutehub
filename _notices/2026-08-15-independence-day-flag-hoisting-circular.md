The Institute will celebrate the 79th Independence Day on **15th August 2026**. The National Flag will be unfurled by the Director at **08:30 AM** in front of the Main Administrative Building, followed by the Director's address and felicitation of campus awards. All faculty, staff, and students with their families are cordially invited.
