The Information Technology Services (ITS) team will conduct a major core optical fiber switch migration and Wi-Fi 6 AP upgrade across the North Campus Academic Blocks and Hostels 1 through 6 on **Saturday, 29th August 2026 between 01:00 AM and 05:00 AM IST**.

During this window, internal intranet access, academic web services, and high-speed Wi-Fi links will experience transient interruptions. Critical server failover clusters in the primary datacenter will remain operational.
