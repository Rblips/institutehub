The Senate in its 48th meeting has ratified revisions to Ordinance Section 6.4 governing the submission and defense timeline for Master of Technology and Master of Science research dissertations. Plagiarism threshold checks have been revised to require less than 10% similarity on standard academic indexing tools prior to defense scheduling.
