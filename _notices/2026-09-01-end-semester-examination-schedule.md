The Mid-Semester Examination schedule for Autumn Semester 2026 has been finalized. Examinations will be conducted in two shifts daily (Morning: 09:30 AM - 11:30 AM; Afternoon: 02:30 PM - 04:30 PM) commencing from **12th October 2026**.

Students must carry their physical Institute Smart ID cards to the examination halls. Calculators without wireless connectivity are permitted according to specific course requirements.
