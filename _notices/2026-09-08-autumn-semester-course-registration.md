All registered students for the Autumn Semester 2026–2027 are hereby notified that portal course registration will open from **15th September 2026 at 10:00 AM** and conclude on **22nd September 2026 at 05:00 PM**.

### Important Instructions:
1. Students must clear any outstanding library dues or hostel fee arrears before accessing the course selection console.
2. Ensure all prerequisite courses are satisfied as listed in the academic curriculum handbook.
3. Maximum credit limits: 24 credits for B.Tech students; 18 credits for M.Tech/MS students.
4. Add/Drop period will remain open until 30th September 2026.
